Slide 1 of 4 · The Problem

The Silent Crisis of Data Leakage

In 2023, Princeton researchers uncovered catastrophic data leakage across 294 published papers in 17 fields. Models repeatedly reported 100% test accuracy because identical twins (same patient, device, or recording) were naively split into both training and test sets.

In traditional 2D flat screens, this failure mode remains invisible until live models collapse in production.

Slide 2 of 4 · The Spatial Solution

Hands-First Spatial Review

Twin Trace makes data leakage visceral. In a 4-minute seated WebXR experience:

  • Pinch & Trace: An illuminating optical trajectory connects duplicate samples across partitions.
  • Physical Quarantine: Drop leaky entities into the quarantine gate to trigger group-disjoint repair.
  • The Unseen Audit: Unseal the blind audit tray to reveal true chance baseline (30%), proving the repair prevented disaster.
